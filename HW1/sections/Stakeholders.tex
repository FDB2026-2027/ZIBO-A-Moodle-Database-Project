\section{Users and Stakeholders of the System}

The users of the system are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Students:}
    Students use the system to access lecture resources, such as slides, submit assignments, complete chapter quizzes, and communicate with classmates about knowledge and information.

    \item \textbf{Teachers:}
    Teachers use the system to publish new notifications, share teaching materials, announce the rules of the final exam, and update the latest information about their lectures.

    \item \textbf{Operations officers:}
    Operations officers are responsible for the daily maintenance of the education system to make sure it can be used normally.
\end{itemize}
