\section{Objectives of the System}
%Brief description of the overall objectives of your system.
%What your system will be used for? Why is it important? What problems does it solve?
With the development of education information technology, students and teachers begin to search for a perfect new solution for both learning and teaching in a more convenient and efficient way. In traditional ways of learning and teaching, although with modern information technology, students must log in to a different distributed management system to acquire fragment information to complete their learning procedures. Also, teachers must publish their teaching messages by personal E-mail, chat groups, etc. to make their students know what they should do next step. It is complex and inefficient for both teachers and students. The Object of our system is to bridge the gap between study management system and students and teachers. To achieve a fast, convenient, and efficient effect in studying and teaching activities.
