\section{Variations to the Requirement Analysis}
We redefined the user model: instead of three separate kinds of user, a single \textit{User} entity is now associated with one or more \textit{Roles} (student, teacher, administrator), and the operation officer of Homework 1 is a user with the administrator role. A user takes part in a course through an \textit{enrollment}, which states whether they participate as a student or as a teacher, while every course has one responsible teacher. We also reorganised the course page into \textit{sections} containing \textit{resources} (files, links and videos), and we record the progress of each student on every resource. The generic ``assignments and quizzes'' are now split into \textit{assignments} with one \textit{submission} per student, and \textit{quizzes} composed of \textit{questions} that students answer through timed \textit{attempts}; a \textit{gradebook} collects the grades of each student in a course. A course forum, \textit{notifications} and an \textit{audit log} support communication and administration, while the QR-code attendance and the enrolment key are left out of this version. The changes are reiterated through all the subsequent steps.
